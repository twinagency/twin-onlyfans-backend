3. CORRECTION SPLIT (the confirmed bug)
 Before: on repeated_recent_line the retry payload was rejected={reason, action, product_id=<the offered product>}, so validate_decision refused that product
 and the snapshot marked it refused_in_this_turn; a valid offer was lost with the wording.
 After: runtime.correction_brief(): WORDING_REASONS (repeated_recent_line, repeated_recent_phrase, invented_policy_claim, content_status_wording, asks_known_name)
 produce kind=wording with product_id=None, the lines to avoid, and keep={action,product_id,sequence} so the offer survives; the product stays offerable.
 Every other recoverable reason is kind=product (product refused, never choosable again, still carries fan_message). Format failures are kind=format.
 Wording checks now run LAST in validate_decision, so a product failure always outranks a wording failure. A wording rewrite that smuggles in a bad product
 is still refused as a product failure (tested). A second failure still goes to a person once (moxy_correction_failed case), no loop.
 Bug fixed during testing: the three new wording reasons were not in RECOVERABLE_REASONS, so no correction was queued at all.

4. NAME, STATUS, REPETITION
 Name: usable_fan_name prefers verified memory, a name he chose, a real name, then profile display name; handles, ids, initials, labels (u12345, Redddd1, YC, C. A. A., @x) are unusable.
 No usable name: nothing is sent and the writer is told never to ask; a draft that asks a KNOWN name is refused (asks_known_name); asking when no name is known is not blocked.
 Status: a sent, unpaid, priced card cannot be called opened/seen/enjoyed/owned; his own "i just opened it" is accepted as evidence; paid or free media unaffected.
 Repetition: a shared run of 5+ words with 2+ content words, or the same 4+ word opening in two longer messages. Pet names (babe, baby, bestie, daddy ...), common words and emoji are ignored.
 The approved chatter bubbles themselves are asserted to pass the detectors (so the approved voice is never blocked).

10. RISKS AND OPEN QUESTIONS
 - The after-transcripts are scripted; real-model quality (does it actually answer the clarification in voice?) is unproven until the real replay above is run.
 - Selector is keyword based (regex signals on the last 3 fan messages); a fan phrasing that matches no signal falls back to the creator-fit defaults (still 2 to 3 excerpts).
 - repeated_recent_phrase is stricter than before: a model that keeps reusing a 5-word phrase gets one correction then a person (moxy_correction_failed case). Watch the case volume in the test chats.
 - asks_known_name: a draft that asks "whats your name" when a usable name exists is refused. A profile display name counts as usable (e.g. "Matthew Campbell" -> Matthew); a wrong profile name would be used.
 - content_status_wording is regex based (opened/seen/enjoyed/own); it only fires when the last 10 messages hold an unpaid priced outgoing card and he has not said he opened it.
 - Held-back set (#45, #89) is my choice; production shows all 8, so those two are not held out in production, only in the generalisation tests.
 - Explicit excerpts #2/#89 reach only cristina, emma, emma_karter; Amelia gets #6 #70 #4 #64 #55 #45 only. Confirm that is your intent.
 - Prompt is about 2 KB larger (section 6d). Nothing else changed in cost or latency.
 - Creator rules (cfg) and all settings are untouched; no schema change, so rollback is simply redeploying 83074e1.
